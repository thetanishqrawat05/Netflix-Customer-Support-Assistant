Netflix Help Center — Communication Standards and Brand Voice (Internal Support Reference)

Tone and voice guidelines for customer support
Netflix support communication should be warm, clear, and solution-oriented, never robotic or overly formal. Agents and automated assistants should acknowledge the customer's frustration briefly, then move quickly to a concrete next step. Avoid technical jargon unless the customer uses it first. Never blame the customer for an issue. Use plain, friendly language, for example: "Let's get this sorted out" rather than "Your request has been received and will be processed."

Scope of support assistance
Netflix support assistants are authorized to help with: billing questions, plan changes, account access and security, streaming and playback troubleshooting, parental controls, and general how-to questions about using the Netflix app or website. Support assistants are NOT authorized to: make legal commitments, discuss unreleased content or business strategy, share internal financial information, or guarantee refunds outside documented policy.

Escalation policy
If a customer's issue cannot be resolved with documented self-service steps, or if the customer explicitly requests a human representative, the assistant should offer to connect them with a live agent or direct them to the phone/chat support line listed on the Help Center contact page. Billing disputes involving suspected fraud should always be escalated to a human agent immediately.

Refund policy summary
Netflix does not provide prorated refunds for unused portions of a monthly billing cycle as a standard policy. Exceptions may be made at the discretion of customer service in cases of billing errors, such as being charged after a cancellation was already processed, or duplicate charges due to a technical error. Any refund exception must be confirmed by checking the account's billing history for the specific error before being offered to the customer.

Accessibility statement
Netflix is committed to making its service usable by people of all abilities. This includes audio description tracks, closed captioning in multiple languages, screen-reader-friendly interfaces on supported platforms, and keyboard navigation support on the web app. Customers requesting accessibility accommodations should be directed to the Accessibility section of the Help Center.

Data handling disclaimer for support interactions
Support assistants should never ask customers to share full payment card numbers, passwords, or two-step verification codes in chat. Account verification should rely on alternate means such as confirming the last four digits of a payment method, billing postal code, or recent invoice amounts.
